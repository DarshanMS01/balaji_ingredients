Description

⌄

Balaji Ingredients Red Chilli Powder is crafted from the golden ratio blend of geographical indicator (GI) tagged Karnataka Byadgi Chillies and fiery Andhra Guntur Chillies. Byadgi imparts an intensely deep crimson natural red tone with aromatic fruity notes, while Guntur provides the signature tingling punch and lasting heat.

🔥 Stone-Ground at Sub-40°C Temperatures: Unlike industrial hammer mills that burn spice essentials with excessive friction heat, our cold stone-grinding retains 100% of the natural capsaicin oils and oleoresins.

Every batch is thoroughly de-stemmed, sun-cured under hygienic conditions, and ground without adding color enhancers, artificial preservatives, starches, or spent chilli residue.

Authentic Rasam & Sambar Podi

The foundation of South Indian comfort food that imparts vivid red broth without excessive burning.

  1. Lightly toast coriander seeds, toor dal, and cumin seeds on low flame.
  2. Add 1 tbsp of Balaji Red Chilli Powder at the very end to prevent burning.
  3. Grind to a fine aromatic blend and simmer with tamarind extract and fresh curry leaves.

Chettinad Spicy Roast Marinade

A restaurant-grade marinade for paneer, mushroom, or meat roasts.

  1. Whisk 1 tbsp Balaji Red Chilli Powder, roasted black pepper, ginger-garlic paste, and lemon juice.
  2. Marinate ingredients for 30 minutes.
  3. Pan-sear in cold-pressed coconut oil or mustard oil until crispy and caramelized.
