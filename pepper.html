Chettinad Pepper Masala Roast

The crown jewel of Chettinad culinary mastery with a fiery, aromatic black pepper crust.

  1. Dry roast whole Balaji Malabar peppercorns, fennel seeds, and cumin on low flame until fragrant.
  2. Coarsely pound in a stone mortar (avoid fine powder for the signature rustic bite).
  3. Sauté with shallots, garlic, and curry leaves in cold-pressed sesame oil before tossing with paneer, mushrooms, or meat.

Traditional Malabar Milagu Rasam

A soothing, immunity-rejuvenating broth perfect for rainy days and respiratory wellness.

  1. Crush 1.5 tsp black peppercorns and 1 tsp cumin seeds with 4 garlic cloves.
  2. Simmer crushed spices in tamarind extract, chopped tomatoes, and fresh coriander stems.
  3. Temper with mustard seeds and curry leaves in golden cow ghee; serve piping hot.
