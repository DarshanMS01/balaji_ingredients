INFRASTRUCTURE

How We Operate

This page outlines how Balaji Ingredients operates across sourcing, processing, packaging, storage and dispatch. Detailed infrastructure information will be updated as our operations are formalised and expanded.

⊛
01

Farm-Gate Sourcing & Aggregation

Ingredients are procured directly from certified agricultural producer clusters across Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Andhra Pradesh. We conduct rigorous pre-procurement batch sampling, grain moisture verification, and foreign matter screening at the collection center before loading.

Direct APMC & Farmer Aggregation
On-site Moisture Testing (<11%)
Traceable Lot Numbering
⊙
02

Precision Cleaning & Sortex Processing

Our processing lines feature multi-stage destoners, gravity separators, and advanced Buhler optical Sortex color sorters delivering 99.5%+ product purity. Pulses undergo calibrated dehusking, splitting, and grading, while whole grains are graded by sieve mesh size to maintain consistent physical characteristics.

Trichromatic Color Sorting
Ferrous & Non-ferrous Metal Detection
Uniform Grain Sizing
◻
03

Industrial Packaging & Private Labeling

We supply high-barrier, food-grade packaging formats tailored for institutional processors, commercial mills, and FMCG brands. Options range from 25kg / 50kg multi-layer woven polypropylene (PP) sacks with inner LDPE liners to 500kg – 1000kg FIBC Jumbo bulk tote bags with vacuum-seal capabilities.

25kg / 50kg Heavy-Duty PP Sacks
1000kg FIBC Bulk Bags
Custom Private Label Stenciling
◫
04

Climate-Controlled & Fumigated Warehousing

Our dedicated central storage facilities operate under controlled temperature (below 22°C) and relative humidity (under 60%) to preserve grain vitality, prevent aflatoxin development, and eliminate weevil infestation. Palletized racking ensures strict First-In, First-Out (FIFO) inventory turnover.

3,500+ MT Palletized Capacity
Scheduled Prophylactic Fumigation
Digital ERP Inventory Tracking
⇢
05

Logistics, Transit & Fast Fleet Dispatch

We manage pan-India logistics coordination through contracted GPS-enabled freight carriers and rail freight for bulk volumes. All shipments are accompanied by digital Goods Consignment Notes (LR), e-Way bills, Certificates of Analysis (CoA), and dispatch weight slips from certified weighbridges.

24 to 48-Hour Regional Dispatch
Full Truckload (FTL) & Part Truckload (PTL)
Certified Weighbridge Slips
